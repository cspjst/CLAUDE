## Conventions
- **Version names** (Claude's proposal, for Jeremy's call): a document's first version keeps its plain name (`code-of-conduct.md`); each later version gets a suffix (`code-of-conduct-v2.md`, or `-v0.N` for drafts like the model). **Readers take the highest version.** Instructions point at the plain name, meaning "the latest version of it".
- Placeholders in templates: `<PROJECT>`, `<DEPT>`, `<HUMAN>`, `<date>`.
